# 8. CURRENT GENERAL AND SPECIFIC OBJECTIVES

## General Objective

> **The primary goal of this study is to develop and evaluate a lightweight Computer Vision framework that determines whether explicit OCR-derived page-level spatial-layout cues provide complementary information for detecting AI-generated text-rich images across varying text-density levels.**

## Specific Objectives

1. **Data**
   > To prepare a controlled dataset of real and AI-generated text-rich images from the TextRich benchmark by applying integrity checks, duplicate and near-duplicate controls, deterministic balanced sampling, and fixed training, validation, and test partitions across six structured image categories.

2. **Computational method**
   > To implement and compare a lightweight visual-only detector, OCR-derived layout-only representations, and a visual-layout fusion model using page-level geometric features and normalized binary spatial-layout maps while excluding recognized text semantics from the layout input.

3. **Prototype**
   > To design and develop a research prototype that integrates image preprocessing, OCR text-region detection, layout representation, authenticity classification, and presentation of prediction and diagnostic outputs for text-rich images.

4. **Evaluation**
   > To evaluate the computational models using accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, macro-F1, and ROC-AUC, analyze performance by image category and training-derived text-density level, and assess the prototype through functional test cases and hardware-specific inference-performance measurements.

Important proposal-editing rule:
- Do not rewrite Objective 2 as “to improve detection.”
- Evaluation language is intentional.
- If adviser wants ISO/IEC 25010 or user evaluation, add only after explicit discussion; do not automatically create human-participant overhead.
